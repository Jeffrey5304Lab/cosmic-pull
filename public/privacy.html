Privacy Policy — Cosmic Pull

Last updated: 15 August 2026

Cosmic Pull ("the game", "we") is a casual pull-the-pin puzzle game. We do not operate any servers and we never receive your personal data. The game does, however, include an optional rewarded video ad supplied by Google AdMob, and ad networks do collect data. This page explains exactly what happens, and what you can do about it.

1. Data we collect ourselves

None. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no tracking by us. We have no servers, so no information about you is ever sent to us.

2. What stays on your device

Your progress — which levels you have unlocked, your best star rating on each level, your stardust balance, the sky themes you own and have selected, and your sound on/off preference — is saved locally on your device using standard on-device storage (localStorage). It never leaves your device and we cannot see it. If you delete the app, this data is removed.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game offers an optional rewarded video ad: you may choose to watch a short video to earn in-game stardust. You never have to watch an ad — the entire game is playable and completable without it.

These ads are served by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested or shown, Google may collect and process:

  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android) and device identifiers
  • Device and app information (device model, operating system, app version, language)
  • Coarse/approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Ad interaction data (that an ad was shown, viewed or clicked)

This data is handled by Google under its own policies, not ours. Please see the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Your controls: you can limit or reset ad personalisation at any time in your device settings — iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and Apple Advertising; Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads. You can also opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ad Settings. Opting out does not remove ads; it makes them non-personalised.

4. Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we present a consent prompt (via Google's User Messaging Platform) before personalised ads are shown, as required by the GDPR and the ePrivacy Directive. You may grant or refuse consent, and you can change your choice at any time from the in-game settings. If you refuse, you will only be shown non-personalised ads (or none at all).

Our legal basis for processing advertising data is your consent. You have the right to access, rectify, erase and restrict processing of your personal data, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Because we hold no data ourselves, requests relating to advertising data should be directed to Google.

5. California residents (CCPA/CPRA)

We do not sell or share personal information for money. Advertising identifiers used for personalised advertising may be considered "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising under California law. You may opt out using the device-level controls described in section 3. We do not knowingly process the personal information of users under 16 for advertising purposes.

6. Children

Cosmic Pull is suitable for all ages, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children. We do not direct the app primarily at children under 13. If the app is used by a child, or is distributed in a child-directed programme, ads are configured to be non-personalised and treated as child-directed in line with COPPA and Google Play's Families policy. If you believe a child has provided personal information through an ad, please contact us and we will assist in having it removed.

7. Network use

The puzzle gameplay itself runs entirely on your device and needs no internet connection. A network connection is used only when you choose to watch a rewarded ad (and, in the regions above, to show the consent prompt).

8. Changes

If a future update changes what is described here, we will update this page and the "last updated" date before that version ships.

9. Contact

Questions? Email mngutoysports@gmail.com.

In short: your progress stays on your phone and we receive nothing about you. The only data collection comes from Google AdMob, and only if you choose to watch an optional rewarded ad.
