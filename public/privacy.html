Privacy Policy — Cosmic Pull

Last updated: 12 August 2026

Cosmic Pull ("the game", "we") is a casual pull-the-pin puzzle game. We designed it to collect no personal data at all. This page explains exactly what the game does and does not do with information.

1. Data we collect

None. Cosmic Pull does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers.

2. What stays on your device

Your progress — which levels you have unlocked, your best star rating on each level, and your sound on/off preference — is saved locally on your device using standard on-device storage (localStorage). It never leaves your device and we cannot see it. If you delete the app, this data is removed.

3. Network use

The game runs entirely on your device and does not require an internet connection to play. It does not send data to us or to any third party.

4. Children

Cosmic Pull is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

5. Changes

If a future update introduces anything that changes this policy (for example optional rewarded ads), we will update this page and the "last updated" date before that version ships.

6. Contact

Questions? Email mngutoysports@gmail.com.

In short: Cosmic Pull keeps your progress on your phone and collects nothing about you.
